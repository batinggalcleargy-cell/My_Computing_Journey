LESSONS 1–6

Computing topics and takeaways.

A quick review of computing ideas, computer components, history, and digital safety.

Lesson summary table

These are sample summaries based on common Introduction to Computing topics. Check Lessons 1, 2, and 6 against your actual class notes and revise them if your instructor used different titles or concepts.

LessonTopicSummary
1Introduction to ComputingComputing involves using computer systems to process information and solve problems. It connects hardware, software, data, and people.
2Computer SystemsA computer system combines physical components and programs to perform tasks. Input, processing, output, and storage work together to handle data.
3History of ComputingComputers developed from early counting tools to powerful modern devices. Each generation brought improvements in size, speed, reliability, and capability.
4Computer HardwareHardware refers to the physical parts of a computer. The processor, memory, storage, and input/output devices work together to run tasks.
5Computer SecurityComputer security helps protect devices, accounts, and information from unauthorized access. Safe habits reduce the risks of scams, malware, and data loss.
6Software and ApplicationsSystem software helps operate and manage a computer, while application software helps users complete specific tasks. Both are important for making a computer useful.

LESSON 3

A short computing timeline

  1. AbacusAn early tool used for counting and arithmetic.
  2. Mechanical calculatorsMachines designed to perform calculations.
  3. ENIAC (1940s)An early general-purpose electronic digital computer.
  4. Transistors and integrated circuitsMade computers smaller, more reliable, and more efficient.
  5. Personal computersBrought computing into homes, schools, and offices.
  6. Smartphones and cloud computingMade computing and online services available almost anywhere.

LESSON 4

Basic computer parts

  • Monitor: displays text, pictures, and other visual output.
  • System unit: contains important components such as the CPU, memory, and storage.
  • Keyboard: enters text, numbers, and commands.
  • Mouse: points, clicks, and selects items on the screen.

LESSON 5

Three simple security tips

🔐

Use strong passwords

Create unique passwords for important accounts and never share them with other people.

🎣

Check suspicious links

Verify the sender and website before clicking links or downloading unexpected attachments.

🔄

Keep software updated

Install trusted updates to receive improvements and security fixes.
